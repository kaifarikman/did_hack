What should we call the researcher?

An autonomous robot that walks into unknown ground, forms a hunch, tests it with its own wheels, writes down what it learned, and always makes it home before the battery runs out.

Recommendation

Two marks that say it in one glance

Both carry the one idea that makes this robot different from a vacuum cleaner with a map: it goes out to learn and it comes back with the answer.

Pick 01

cairn

Marks the way. Finds its way back.

A cairn is the stack of stones explorers leave to mark a trail and a way home. Each stone is a confirmed finding; the pile only grows with evidence. Strongest silhouette of the set at 16px.

Pick 02

Tern

Goes far. Always comes home.

The arctic tern makes the longest round trip of any animal and never loses the way back. Light, friendly, and the mark animates beautifully: wings draw out, home dot lands.

From shortlist to mark

  1. Star two or three names, say each out loud in the pitch sentence: “Meet ___, an AI researcher that…”.
  2. Run a trademark and domain check on the finalists; the “known uses” column is a starting point, not a search.
  3. Refine the chosen mark on a grid, draw the wordmark in GT Standard L, and export favicon, app icon and single-color versions.
